# FieldShift – one-page summary
**Problem:** Sowing dates and crop choices were set for a past climate. Farmers face more heat, less predictable rain and shrinking water margins, but satellite data is hard to turn into decisions.
**Solution:** FieldShift converts NASA POWER climate records and NASA GIBS imagery into plain farm decisions: what changed, which crops still fit, when to sow, how much water to plan, which risks to watch.
**NASA data:** POWER (temperature, rainfall 2000–2025), GIBS (MODIS true colour/NDVI/land temperature, IMERG rain rate).
**Key features:** climate-shift analyser · transparent crop scoring · adapted planting calendar · water planner · risk alerts · what-if simulator · farm health score · Farmer mode · English/Urdu · printable report.
**Impact:** food security through better crop/timing choices, water savings through planned irrigation, and early warning for heat and drought.
**Limits:** monthly data, generic crop rules, simplified ET₀ – decision support, not a forecast.
