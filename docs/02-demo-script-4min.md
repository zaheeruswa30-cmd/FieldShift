# 4-minute demo script
**0:00–0:25 Hook:** "The fields haven't moved. The climate has." Show the globe, click Launch.
**0:25–1:00 Overview:** Pick the region. Point out the Live NASA badge, the health score, and the KPIs.
**1:00–1:45 Evidence:** Map: switch true colour → NDVI → land temperature, play the date slider. Then Climate shift: "the last five years are X°C warmer than 2000–09."
**1:45–2:30 Decisions:** Crop suitability (show the stacked score breakdown), then the planting calendar: traditional vs adapted windows.
**2:30–3:20 What-if:** Drag warming to +2°C and rain to −20%. Watch scores, risks and recommendations change.
**3:20–3:45 Reach:** Toggle Farmer mode and Urdu; show Ctrl K custom location and the printed report.
**3:45–4:00 Close:** Data sources, honest limits, next steps.
